# v1.0.20 科技树全选与截图汇总

- 在“自动补位时避开折叠载具”右侧新增全选按钮，再次点击可取消全选。手机端位于筛选面板内。
- 全选覆盖当前国家、军种的常规研发载具，包含折叠载具，不受搜索词影响；不会自动选入金币、联队或隐藏载具。
- 取消全选保留已拥有标记和填写的研发进度，不影响其他国家或军种。
- 导出科技树截图的顶部、底部显示常规科技树原始总费用，以及当前选择实际还需的 RP 和银狮。
- 缺失价格会提示“已知数值”；收录对照不可用时不显示虚假的完整总额。
- 统一全选与自动补位方框、文字的尺寸和对齐。修复复选框点击后残留绿色外框，保留键盘焦点提示。

本次不修改载具价格、配件数据或精确规划算法。游戏版本仍显示 2.59.0.38，既有数据修正范围不变。

## 下载

Windows 用户下载 `WarThunderResearchCalculator-v1.0.20-portable.zip`。退出旧版，解压后运行 `WarThunderResearchCalculator.exe`，无需另装 Node.js。不带 `portable` 的 ZIP 为源码包。

## English

- Added Select all beside the autofill option. Click again to deselect. On mobile, both controls are in the filter panel.
- Selection covers the current nation's regular research tree, including foldered vehicles. Search filters do not limit it. Premium, squadron, and hidden vehicles are excluded.
- Deselecting keeps owned marks and saved research progress. Other trees are unaffected.
- Exported screenshots show original tree totals and the remaining RP and Silver Lions for the current selection at both ends.
- Missing prices are flagged. Unavailable roster data is not shown as a complete total.
- Aligned the checkboxes and labels. Removed the green ring left by mouse clicks while keeping keyboard focus indicators.

Vehicle prices, modification data, and the route optimization algorithm are unchanged.
